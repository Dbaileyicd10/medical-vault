Medical Vault Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Medical Vault is made by Generations Arising. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the Medical Vault app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The short version

Your records belong to you. We do not collect, see, sell, or share any of the information you enter. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What you store in the app

You can enter lab results, medications, medical history, doctors' contact details, and the names of the people you keep records for. This information is stored:

  • On your device, and
  • In your own private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, so your records stay in sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Information in your private iCloud database is protected by Apple and is only available to devices signed in to your Apple Account. We, the developer, cannot access it. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple protects iCloud data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else. It contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card details. The app only checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active.

Backups you create

If you use Export Backup, the app creates a file containing your records and saves it wherever you choose (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). You control that file. Anyone you share it with can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Sharing a summary

The Share button creates a text summary of one person's medications and recent lab results. It is sent only where you choose to send it, such as a message to your doctor.

Deleting your information

You can delete any lab result, medication, or history entry in the app, or remove a person and all of their records from Manage People. Deleting the app removes its records from that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, delete the records in the app before removing it, or manage iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

Children

The app is meant to be used by adults, including to keep records for family members. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Medical Vault is a personal record keeper. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice. Normal ranges differ between labs; always go by the range on your own report and talk with your doctor about your results.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

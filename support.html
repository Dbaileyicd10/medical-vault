Common questions

How do I add lab work quickly?
Tap Add Lab on the Home screen. Choose the date once, then step through each test: type a result and tap Next, or tap Skip. Tap Finish any time, then review and save.

How do I record vital signs?
Tap Add Vitals on the Home screen or the Vitals tab. The date and time are filled in for right now, so you can record several times a day. Enter any of blood pressure, pulse, temperature, blood sugar, weight, and pulse ox, choose the details (which arm, sitting or standing, fasting or after a meal), and tap Save.

How do I find one specific test?
In Add Lab, choose Pick Tests and search by name (for example "BNP"). While entering results, tap the counter at the top right to search all tests.

My test isn't in the list.
On the Labs tab, tap + and choose New Type of Lab Test. You can also tap Test not in the list? Add it while adding lab work.

The normal range on my report is different.
Labs use different ranges. While entering a result, open Range on this report and type the range printed on your report. The app remembers it for next time.

How do I keep records for a family member?
Tap the name (or photo) at the top left, then Manage People, then Add Person.

How do I add a photo for a person?
Tap the name at the top left, then Manage People, tap the person, then Add Photo and choose a picture. The photo then shows at the top of each screen in place of the name. You can change or remove it the same way.

The text is too big or too small.
Open the menu (☰) → Text Size and choose Extra Small, Small, Medium, Large, or Match iPhone Setting.

How do I see what the app looks like with records in it?
Open ☰ → Manage People → Add Sample Person. Everything for Sample Patient is made up. Tap Remove Sample on the Home screen when you're done; your own records aren't touched.

How do my records get to my other devices?
Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Medical Vault on each iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Records sync on their own. You can check iCloud status in the menu (☰).

One device shows different records or a result twice.
Open ☰ → Sync. Tap Remove Duplicate Lab Results on one device only; your other devices update through iCloud in a minute or two. If a device still looks different, tap Re-download from iCloud on that device, then quit and reopen the app.

How do I back up or move my records?
Open the menu (☰) and tap Export Backup to save a file. On another device, tap Restore from Backup and choose that file. Restoring never creates duplicates.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the menu (☰) → Subscription → Manage or Cancel Subscription. You can also go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and your records stay yours.

Is my information private?
Yes. It stays on your devices and in your private iCloud account. We can't see it. Read the privacy policy.
